The one-minute version

Everything you'd want to change lives at roarrealty.in/studio. Sign in with Google, edit, press Publish. The live website updates in about ten seconds. You never touch code, and you cannot break the site from there.

How editing works

  1. Open the item you want to change and type. Your work saves automatically as a draft as you go — nothing is lost if you close the tab.
  2. Press the green Publish button at the bottom of the screen. Until you do, changes are invisible to visitors.
  3. Wait about ten seconds and refresh the website. Your change is live.
You can't break anything

Sanity keeps a full history of every document. If you delete a paragraph you wanted, open the item, click the ⋯ menu at the top and choose Review changes to see previous versions and restore one.

Your phone, RERA number & contact details

These are blank right now, on purpose. Filling them in is the most important thing you'll do in this guide.

Why they're blank

The original design carried invented placeholders — the phone number +91 98100 00000 and the RERA registration HRERA-GGM-XXXX-2026. Neither is real. A fake phone number that looks real is far more dangerous than an obvious gap, because it gets published and nobody notices until a buyer dials it. So the website shows a visible ⚠ warning marker wherever a detail is missing, and those markers disappear the moment you fill each field in.

How to fill them in

  1. Open Studio → Site settings. The Contact tab is showing by default.
  2. Phone — the number you want people to call. Write it how you'd want it read, for example +91 98765 43210.
  3. WhatsApp number — must include the country code and no spaces: +919876543210. This powers the green WhatsApp button that floats on every page and the WhatsApp tab on the mobile bar. If you leave it blank, those buttons stay hidden.
  4. Email — for example hello@roarrealty.in.
  5. Office address — your registered office, as you'd want it printed.
  6. RERA agent registration number — switch to the Legal tab. Enter it exactly as issued. It appears in your footer and on the disclaimer page.
  7. Registered entity name and CIN — also on the Legal tab, used in the copyright line.
  8. Social profiles — back on Contact, press Add item, choose the platform, paste the full URL.
  9. Press Publish. Refresh the site — the ⚠ markers are gone.
Please read before publishing the site

In India, advertising property brokerage without displaying a valid RERA agent registration number is an offence under the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016. This applies in both Haryana and Uttar Pradesh, where your listings are. The site should not be made public until a genuine registration number is in this field. Section 9 covers the order to do things in.

Replacing the logo

Adding a property

Roughly five minutes per listing once you have the photos ready.

  1. Studio → Properties → All properties, then the ＋ button at the top.
  2. Type the property name. Then click Generate under URL slug — that becomes the web address.
  3. Choose the type — Apartments, Villas, Off-plan, Commercial or Plots. This drives the search filters.
  4. Enter the price in crore. This one catches people out: the box wants crore, not rupees. 85 lakh is 0.85. 14.5 crore is 14.5. Leave it blank and tick “Price on request” if you'd rather not show it.
  5. Write a short summary — one or two sentences. It appears on cards and in Google results.
  6. Fill the Specifications tab — bedrooms, bathrooms, carpet area, possession, amenities.
  7. Add photos on the Photos & media tab. Drag them all in at once. The first is the card image; drag to reorder.
  8. Write alt text for each photo. It's required, and it's how blind visitors and Google understand the image. “Living room with floor-to-ceiling windows” is plenty.
  9. Complete the Glass File tab — tick only what has genuinely been verified. See the warning below.
  10. Set the Location tab — city, locality, and drop a map pin if you have one.
  11. Press Publish. It appears on the site in about ten seconds.

The fields that matter most

Price (₹ crore) important
In crore. 0.85 = 85 lakh. 14.5 = 14.5 crore.
Carpet area
The area the buyer actually occupies. This is what your pricing promise is built on — always fill it.
Super built-up area
Optional, but powerful. Enter both and the page automatically calculates and displays the percentage the buyer isn't getting. It's the clearest demonstration of your carpet-area rule on the whole site.
Badge
The small label on the photo — “Ultra-luxury”, “Escrow-protected”, “Pre-leased 7.1%”.
Feature on homepage
Puts it in the six-property grid on the front page.
Illustrative sample
Marks a listing as placeholder. Shows a “Sample” badge and hides it from Google. All nine current listings have this ticked.
Price breakdown
Itemise every charge in plain language. Renders as a clean table on the listing page.
Brochure
Upload a PDF and the page offers it as a download — but asks for a phone number first, so each download becomes a lead.
Only tick what you've actually checked

The Glass File panel deliberately shows unverified items as not ticked rather than hiding them. A due-diligence panel that only ever shows green ticks tells a buyer nothing, and would be exactly the opacity your business exists to replace. An honest partial file is far more persuasive than a suspiciously perfect one.

Testimonials, team & builders

Testimonials

Studio → Testimonials. Three placeholder quotes are in there now, each ticked as Illustrative sample. Replace them with real ones, then untick that box.

Get written consent first

Publishing invented client quotes as though they were real falls foul of the ASCI code and the misleading-advertisement provisions of the Consumer Protection Act. Before a testimonial goes live, get the client's permission in writing — a WhatsApp message saying “yes, happy for you to use that” is enough, and worth keeping.

The same applies to the claims on your homepage: “12 years”, “4,200 keys handed over” and “100% escrow-protected”. Confirm each is literally true, or edit it in Studio → Homepage.

Team & agents

Studio → Team & agents. Name, role, photo, a short bio, and their individual RERA registration number if they hold one. They appear on your About page in the order you set under “Display order” — lower numbers come first.

Builders

Add a developer once — name, logo, year established, projects delivered, and a short note on their track record — and then attach them to any listing from that property's Location tab. Their record then shows on the property page, which supports the due-diligence story rather than just asserting it.

Localities

Each locality becomes its own page, for example roarrealty.in/in/gurugram/golf-course-road. Those pages are how someone searching “property in Golf Course Road” finds you. Add a short intro and, if you have time, a proper area guide in the body field — the more genuinely useful that page is, the higher it ranks.

8

Your leads inbox

Every enquiry from every form on the site lands in two places at once: your email, and a private inbox inside Studio.

What happens when someone enquires

  1. They submit a form — the contact page, a Glass File request, a site-visit request, a brochure download, or the exit popup.
  2. You get an email within seconds, with their name, number, which property they were looking at, and buttons to call or WhatsApp them straight away.
  3. They get an automatic reply in your brand's voice, confirming a shortlist is coming within 48 hours.
  4. The enquiry is stored in your private database, so a lost email is never a lost lead.

Working the inbox

Studio → Leads. It asks for a password the first time — a separate one from your Google sign-in, because this data is more sensitive than your website content.

Where to find the leads password

It's deliberately not printed in this document, since a PDF gets forwarded. Find it at vercel.com → your project → Settings → Environment Variables → the value of LEADS_ADMIN_PASSWORD. Click the eye icon to reveal it. You can change it there to something memorable — edit the value, save, and redeploy.

Once you're in, each lead can be:

  • Moved between New → Contacted → Qualified → Closed with one click.
  • Annotated with private notes that only your team sees.
  • Messaged on WhatsApp instantly via the link on the right of each card.
  • Searched by name, phone, email, property or message content.
  • Exported — Export CSV downloads everything currently on screen, ready for Excel or a CRM.

Spam protection

Three defences are already running without any setup: a hidden field that only automated bots fill in, a timing check that rejects anything submitted impossibly fast, and rate limits of three enquiries per minute and twenty-five per day from a single visitor. Section 10 covers an optional fourth layer.

On privacy

Enquiries are stored in a private database, never in the public content system, and visitors' IP addresses are stored as a one-way hash rather than the address itself. Your published privacy policy reflects exactly this, and commits you to never selling or sharing enquirers' details.

Going live: removing preview mode

One setting takes the site public. Everything on the checklist below should be done before you flip it.

What preview mode is doing right now

  • Telling Google and every other search engine not to index any page.
  • Serving an empty sitemap and a Disallow: / instruction to crawlers.
  • Showing the “Preview mode · sample content” bar at the top of every page.
  • Keeping every illustrative sample listing out of search results.

Pre-flight checklist

Before you go public
☐Real RERA agent registration number is in Site settings legally required
☐Real phone, WhatsApp, email and office address are filled in
☐Your real listings are published
☐The nine illustrative samples are deleted or corrected
☐Testimonials are real, with written consent — or the placeholders are deleted
☐The “12 years” and “4,200 keys” claims are confirmed accurate
☐Properties → Missing photos is empty
☐The domain fix in section 10.2 is done

The switch

  1. Go to vercel.com and open your roar-realty project.
  2. Click Settings, then Environment Variables in the left menu.
  3. Find NEXT_PUBLIC_LAUNCH_MODE. Click Edit.
  4. Change the value from preview to live. Save.
  5. Go to the Deployments tab, click the ⋯ on the most recent deployment, and choose Redeploy.
  6. Wait about two minutes, then load the site. The preview bar is gone and you're public.
It's reversible

If you need to pull the site back out of public view, set the same variable to preview and redeploy. Do note that Google may already have indexed pages by then, and those take a while to drop out again.

Immediately afterwards

  1. Tell Google the site exists. Go to search.google.com/search-console, choose Domain, enter roarrealty.in, and add the TXT record it gives you at your domain registrar. Verify.
  2. Submit your sitemap — in Search Console, open Sitemaps and enter sitemap.xml.
  3. Check a property page in Google's Rich Results Test to confirm listings are readable as structured data.
10

Setup still outstanding

Four items. The first two are worth doing this week; the rest are optional improvements.

10.1  Connect the content webhook recommended

Without this, your edits take up to an hour to appear instead of ten seconds. It's a five-minute, one-time setup.

  1. Go to sanity.io/manage and open your project.
  2. API → Webhooks → Create webhook.
  3. URL: https://roarrealty.in/api/revalidate
  4. Dataset: production  ·  Trigger on: tick Create, Update and Delete
  5. Filter:
    _type in ["property","homepage","siteSettings","testimonial","insight","faq","locality","builder","teamMember"]
  6. Projection: {"_type": _type, "slug": slug.current}
  7. HTTP method: POST. Secret: copy the value of SANITY_REVALIDATE_SECRET from Vercel → Settings → Environment Variables.
  8. Save. Publish any change and confirm the site updates within seconds.

10.2  Fix the www / non-www mismatch do before going live

Vercel currently treats www.roarrealty.in as your main address, so roarrealty.in redirects to it. But the site tells Google its official address is roarrealty.in without the www. The two disagree, which sends Google mixed signals about which version to rank.

Harmless while you're private. Fix it in under a minute:

  1. Vercel → your project → Settings → Domains.
  2. Find roarrealty.in (the one without www) and set it as the Primary Domain.
  3. Done — www now redirects to the short address, matching what the site tells Google.

If you'd rather keep www as your public address, that's equally fine — instead change NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL in Vercel to https://www.roarrealty.in and redeploy. They just have to agree.

10.3  Add invisible spam protection optional

Free and unlimited, and unlike the old “click all the traffic lights” puzzles it's invisible to genuine visitors.

  1. Sign up free at dash.cloudflare.com. You do not need to move your domain there.
  2. Open Turnstile in the sidebar → Add widget.
  3. Name it roar-realty, add the hostnames roarrealty.in, www.roarrealty.in and localhost, mode Managed.
  4. Copy the two keys into Vercel → Settings → Environment Variables as NEXT_PUBLIC_TURNSTILE_SITE_KEY and TURNSTILE_SECRET_KEY, then redeploy.

The site detects both keys and switches the protection on by itself. No code change needed.

10.4  A mailbox that can receive mail optional

Your site sends from hello@roarrealty.in, which is already verified and working. But nothing currently receives mail at that address. Zoho Mail's Forever Free plan (zoho.com/mail) gives you five mailboxes on your own domain at no cost — about fifteen minutes of DNS setup. Google Workspace has no free tier any more.

When something looks wrong

What you're seeingWhat's happening
I published a change but the site looks the same Either the webhook in 10.1 isn't connected yet — changes then take up to an hour — or your browser cached the old page. Press Ctrl+Shift+R to force a fresh copy.
A ⚠ warning marker on the live site A contact detail is still blank. Go to Site settings and fill it in — see section 3.
“Sample” badges on my listings The Illustrative sample box is ticked on that property. Untick it and publish.
The WhatsApp button isn't showing The WhatsApp number in Site settings is empty, or missing its country code. It must look like +919876543210.
A property won't appear in search results Check it's published, not just saved as a draft — the Publish button turns grey once there's nothing left to publish.
Studio won't load, or shows a connection error Usually the address you're visiting isn't on Sanity's approved list. sanity.io/manage → your project → API → CORS origins → add it with “Allow credentials” ticked.
No email when a form is submitted Check your spam folder first. Then confirm LEAD_NOTIFY_EMAIL in Vercel points at the right inbox.
The moving 3D backgrounds aren't showing Working as intended. They switch off automatically on older phones, on slow connections, and for anyone who has asked their device to reduce motion.
Google still isn't showing my site Indexing takes days to weeks even after going live. Confirm preview mode is off, then check Search Console for coverage errors.
Restoring something you deleted

Open the item, click ⋯ at the top right, and choose Review changes. You can see every previous version and roll back to any of them. This works for text you've overwritten as well as whole documents.

Settings in Vercel worth knowing

Vercel → your project → Settings → Environment Variables. Changing any of these requires a redeploy to take effect.

NameWhat it does
NEXT_PUBLIC_LAUNCH_MODEThe public/private switch. preview or live.
LEADS_ADMIN_PASSWORDThe password for your leads inbox. Change it to whatever you like.

Please don't change these

Anything containing a long random string — the Sanity tokens, the database address, the Resend key. Those are passwords to the services that keep your site running, and altering one will take the site or your enquiry forms offline until it's put back.
