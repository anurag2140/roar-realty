1

The one thing to understand

Your website has two halves. Knowing which half you are in explains everything else in this guide.

The halfWhat it is
The website
roarrealty.in		 What your customers see. You never edit this directly. It simply displays whatever is in the other half.
Your control panel
roarrealty.in/studio		 Where all the words, photos and settings actually live. You edit here, and the website updates itself.

That separation is why you can change your phone number, add a review or rewrite a headline without anyone technical being involved. It also means there is nothing you can click in the control panel that takes the website offline.

How every change works

  1. Go to roarrealty.in/studio and sign in with Google. Use the same Google account each time.
  2. Find the thing you want to change in the left-hand menu, and click it.
  3. Type. Your work saves automatically as you go, so nothing is lost if you close the tab or your phone dies.
  4. Press the green Publish button at the bottom. Until you do, nobody else can see the change.
  5. Wait about ten seconds, then refresh the website. Done.
Changed your mind?

Open the item, click the … button at the top right, and choose Review changes. You will see every previous version and can put any of them back. This works for a paragraph you overwrote as well as for a whole page you deleted.

On your phone

The control panel works on a phone browser. It is more comfortable on a laptop for long writing, but adding a review or correcting a phone number from your phone is perfectly practical.

How the WhatsApp button behaves

Because you have more than one number, the green WhatsApp button does not guess. Tapping it opens a small list so the visitor picks a desk, and the message is already written for them, including which page they were reading. So instead of "Hi", you receive something like:

Hello Roar Realty, I’d like to discuss a property (from the dubai page).

That tells you what they were looking at before you have said a word, which is worth more than it sounds.

To change or add a number

  1. Site settings → Contact tab.
  2. Edit the number you want to change. Always include the country code and no spaces: +919876543210.
  3. To add a fourth desk, scroll to "Extra WhatsApp desks", press Add item, give it a name (which the visitor sees) and the number.
  4. Press Publish.
The country code matters

A WhatsApp number without its country code will not work. If the button stops opening WhatsApp, this is almost always why. Check there is a + and no spaces or brackets.

Getting reviews, and publishing them

Your website has a full review system at roarrealty.in/reviews. Clients write them, you approve them, they appear.

How it works

  1. A client fills in the form at roarrealty.in/reviews. They give a star rating, their review, where they are based, where they bought, and their email and phone.
  2. It arrives in your control panel under Testimonials, marked with an hourglass and the words AWAITING APPROVAL. It is not on the website yet, and nobody can see it.
  3. You check it. Their email and phone are attached so you can confirm they are genuinely a client of yours.
  4. Tick "Approved for the website" and press Publish. It appears within ten seconds, and the star rating at the top of the reviews page recalculates automatically.
Why this is worth doing properly

Because you verify each one, your reviews page can honestly say every review is checked against your transaction records. No competitor can claim that, and it is exactly the kind of proof your whole brand is built on. Reviews you cannot stand behind would undermine the one thing that makes you different.

Getting your first fifteen, fast

The quickest route is a personal WhatsApp to past clients. Copy this, change the first name, send it one at a time rather than as a broadcast.

Hi [name], hope you’re well.

We’ve just put the new Roar Realty website live and I’m adding a reviews section to it. Would you mind writing a couple of lines about how the process went for you? Honest is better than glowing, if something took longer than you wanted, say that too.

Takes about a minute here: roarrealty.in/reviews

Thank you, it genuinely helps.

A few things that make a real difference to the response rate:

  • Ask individually. A broadcast gets ignored; a personal message gets answered.
  • Ask soon after a handover, while the experience is fresh and the goodwill is high.
  • Say honest is better than glowing. It removes the awkwardness, and the review you get back will be far more persuasive.
  • Send the direct link, roarrealty.in/reviews, not just the homepage.

Adding a review yourself

If a client sends you their words over WhatsApp instead, you can enter it for them: Testimonials → + → fill in the quote, their name, rating, region and where they bought → make sure Approved is ticked → Publish.

Before you publish someone's words

Get their permission, even a WhatsApp message saying "yes, fine to use that" is enough, and keep it. Publishing a named person’s quote without their agreement causes problems that are entirely avoidable by asking.

Until you have real ones

The reviews section does not appear on your homepage at all while it is empty. That reads as a new practice, which is fine. Cards saying "placeholder" would read as carelessness, which is not, so those hide themselves automatically now the site is live.

Writing articles

Seventeen articles are already written and live at roarrealty.in/insights. This is what will bring you visitors from Google who have never heard your name.

Why bother

Someone searching "super built up vs carpet area" or "what does escrow actually cover in Dubai" is a buyer thinking carefully. If your article answers them properly, you have earned the conversation before any competitor knows they exist. It is slow, and it compounds.

Adding one

  1. Insights → the + button.
  2. Type the title, then click Generate under the slug. That becomes the web address.
  3. Write a two-line excerpt. It shows on the listing page and in Google results.
  4. Add a cover image and a category.
  5. Write the article in the body box. You can add headings, lists, links and images as you go.
  6. Publish.
Dates are hidden on purpose

Articles do not show a publication date anywhere on the site. Your pieces are about how markets work rather than this week’s news, and a visible date makes good advice look stale long before it actually is.

What to write about

The pieces that work best for you are the ones that tell buyers something inconvenient. What Dubai’s protections do not cover. Why a delay penalty clause is usually decoration. How to check "only two units left" in ten seconds. That is the voice the existing seventeen are written in, so anything new should sit alongside them comfortably.

Turning on properties

The whole property system is built and working. It is simply switched off until you have real listings to put in it.

What is waiting behind the switch

  • A search page with filters for type, budget, bedrooms, locality, possession status and amenities
  • Individual property pages with photo galleries, floor plans, maps and the Glass File panel
  • Visitors can save properties to a shortlist and compare three side by side
  • A "Request Glass File" button on every listing that becomes an enquiry

To switch it on

  1. Add at least four real properties first. Properties → the + button.
  2. Site settings → Look & effects → tick "Show the properties section".
  3. Publish. The Properties link appears in your menu and the grid appears on the homepage.

Adding a property

About five minutes each, once you have the photos.

Price
In crore, not rupees. 85 lakh is 0.85. 14.5 crore is 14.5. This catches everyone out once.
Carpet area
The area the buyer actually occupies. Always fill this in, it is what your pricing promise rests on.
Super built-up
Optional but powerful. Enter both and the page automatically works out and displays the percentage the buyer is not getting.
Photos
Drag them all in at once. The first is the card image. Alt text is required for each, one short sentence describing the photo.
Glass File
Tick only what has genuinely been verified. Unticked items show as unticked on purpose.
Brochure
Upload a PDF and the page offers it as a download, but asks for a phone number first. Every download becomes a lead.
Useful views

Under Properties you will find Featured on homepage and Missing photos. Check the second one before switching the section on. A listing with no photograph converts very badly.

8

Your enquiries inbox

Every form on the website lands in two places at once: your email, and a private inbox inside the control panel.

What happens when someone enquires

  1. They submit a form, from the contact page, a calculator, the exit popup, or a property.
  2. You get an email within seconds with their name, number, what they were looking at, and buttons to call or WhatsApp them straight away.
  3. They get an automatic reply in your brand’s voice, promising a shortlist within 48 hours.
  4. The enquiry is stored in a private database, so a lost email is never a lost lead.

Working the inbox

Control panel → Leads. It asks for a password the first time, separate from your Google sign-in, because this data is more sensitive than your website words.

Where to find the leads password

Deliberately not printed in this document, since PDFs get forwarded. Go to vercel.com → your project → Settings → Environment Variables and reveal the value of LEADS_ADMIN_PASSWORD. You can change it there to something you will remember.

Each lead can be moved between New, Contacted, Qualified and Closed, annotated with private notes, messaged on WhatsApp in one click, searched, and exported to a spreadsheet with the Export CSV button.

Spam

Three defences run automatically: a hidden field only robots fill in, a timing check that rejects anything submitted impossibly fast, and limits of three enquiries a minute and twenty-five a day from one visitor. You do not need to do anything.

On privacy

Enquiries are stored in a private database, never in the public content system, and visitors’ IP addresses are stored scrambled rather than readable. Your published privacy policy reflects exactly this and commits you to never selling or sharing enquirers’ details.

The free calculators

At roarrealty.in/tools. These are the strongest lead generators on the site, and worth understanding.

The Dubai yield calculator

A visitor enters a purchase price, the advertised rent and the unit size. The calculator then deducts the service charge, void periods, management fee and letting commission, and shows what actually reaches them, alongside the gap between that and the advertised figure.

The moment somebody drags the service-charge slider and watches a 7% yield become 4.2%, they understand your entire argument without you saying a word. A button appears at exactly that point offering to check the real numbers for their specific building. That is the conversion.

The carpet area calculator

The visitor enters the super built-up area, the carpet area and the quoted rate. It shows what they are really paying per square foot of usable space, and what the difference costs them in rupees. Rule 03, turned into arithmetic.

Why these work better than a brochure

Nobody shares an advert. People do share a tool that showed them something alarming about a purchase they were about to make. Both calculators are free, need no email, and are built to be sent to a friend, which is how inbound actually starts.

When something looks wrong

What you're seeingWhat to do
I published a change but the site looks the same Your browser is showing you a saved copy. Press Ctrl+Shift+R (or Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac) to force a fresh one. If it still has not changed after a few minutes, the webhook in section 12 is not connected yet.
The WhatsApp button does nothing The number is missing its country code. It must look like +919876543210, with no spaces.
A review I approved is not showing Check you pressed Publish as well as ticking Approved. Both are needed.
The reviews section is missing from my homepage It hides itself until at least one real review is approved. Add one and it reappears.
The control panel will not load Usually the address you are visiting has not been approved in Sanity. sanity.io/manage → your project → API → CORS origins → add it with "Allow credentials" ticked.
No email when a form is submitted Check your spam folder first. Then confirm LEAD_NOTIFY_EMAIL in Vercel points at the right inbox.
The moving backgrounds are not showing Working as intended. They switch off on older phones, slow connections, and for anyone who has asked their device to reduce motion.
Google is not showing my site Indexing takes days to weeks. Make sure you have completed the Search Console step in section 12.
Getting something back that you deleted

Open the item, click … at the top right, choose Review changes. Every previous version is there and you can roll back to any of them.

Settings that live in Vercel, not the control panel

Vercel → your project → Settings → Environment Variables. Changing any of these needs a redeploy afterwards.

NameWhat it does
LEADS_ADMIN_PASSWORDThe password for your enquiries inbox.
LEAD_NOTIFY_EMAILWhere enquiry alerts are sent. Accepts several addresses separated by commas.
NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_MODENot currently set, and the site is live. Adding it with the value preview pulls the whole site out of Google.
Please do not change these

Anything containing a long random string: the Sanity tokens, the database address, the Resend key. Those are passwords to the services keeping your site running, and altering one takes the website or your enquiry forms offline until it is put back.

A closing note

Everything in sections 1 to 8 is yours to change whenever you like, without a developer and without any risk of breaking the site. The parts that could break it live in Vercel, and this guide has been careful to point out exactly which those are.
